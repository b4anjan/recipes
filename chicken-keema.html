Spicy Chicken Keema

कुखुराको किमा

Ground chicken cooked in a slow-browned onion and tomato masala with ginger, garlic and green chilies. Switch to Nepali style for mustard oil and a finish of timur.

Prep 15 minCook 30 minServes 4Spicy

      Tips

      • Use ground chicken thigh if you can find it. Breast-only mince dries out faster.
      • Don't rush the onions. Deep golden onions are where most of the flavor comes from.
      • The masala is ready when it darkens and the oil shines at the edges of the pan.
      • Cook until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest clump.
      • Keema tastes even better the next day. It keeps 3 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer; reheat with a splash of water.
