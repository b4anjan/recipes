Chipotle-Style Corn Salsa

Sweet corn, red onion, jalapeño and cilantro tossed with fresh lime. Mild, bright, and ready in about 5 minutes.

Prep 5 minCook 0 minServes 6Mild

Ingredients

Tap each one as you gather it
6 servings

    Steps

      Serve it with

      Burrito bowlsOver cilantro-lime rice and beans
      TacosShredded beef, carnitas, or fish
      Grilled fishSalmon or mahi-mahi
      ChipsNext to guacamole on a party tray

      Tips

      • Pick a lime that feels soft and heavy for its size. It gives more juice, and lime carries the flavor.
      • For a mild salsa, remove every jalapeño seed and rib. Leave a few seeds in for more heat.
      • Thaw corn with the cold-water rinse or in the fridge, never on the counter.
      • Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in an airtight container in the fridge.
