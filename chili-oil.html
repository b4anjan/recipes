Nepali-Style Chili Oil

खुर्सानीको तेल

Dried red chilies, golden garlic and whole spices slowly infused into oil, with timur for that Nepali tingle and fresh lemon to finish. Salty and sour, never sweet.

Prep 10 minCook 10 minMakes about 2 cupsHot

Ingredients

1× batch
Heat

    Steps

      Store it in the fridge

      Garlic and fresh lemon sitting in oil at room temperature can grow the bacteria that cause botulism, even in a closed jar. Keep this chili oil cold.

      • Refrigerate at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 4 days.
      • For longer, freeze it in small jars or an ice-cube tray and thaw one portion at a time in the fridge.
      • Always scoop with a clean, dry spoon.

      Serve it with

      MomoThe classic. Spoon it over or dip
      Chow meinStir a spoonful through the noodles
      Dal bhatA little on the side of the plate
      Eggs and fried riceDrizzle on top just before eating

      Tips

      • Keep the heat moderate. Garlic burns quickly and turns the whole batch bitter.
      • Use only a small spoon of sugar. It rounds out the flavor; the oil should stay salty and sour.
      • Use lemon, not lime, for the natural sour finish. White vinegar works too.
      • A fresh cinnamon stick and a cardamom pod dropped in before closing the jar keep the aroma fresh.
